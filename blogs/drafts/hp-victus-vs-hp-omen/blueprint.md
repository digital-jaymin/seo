### H2: Display and Build Quality — What You Actually Notice Day to Day
**Purpose:** Address the practical difference beyond specs  
**Target length:** ~200 words

- **Display:** Both offer 144Hz IPS panels in base configs. Omen offers 165Hz and QHD options. For competitive gaming, the difference matters above 60fps.
- **Build:** Victus uses a full-plastic chassis (lighter, more affordable). Omen uses aluminum on the base — more rigid, premium feel, better heat dissipation through the body. Real-world difference: Omen flexes less, Victus can creak slightly on the deck.
- **Keyboard:** Victus has basic per-zone RGB. Omen Command Center allows per-key RGB and macro customization on higher models.
- **Port selection:** Both include USB-A, USB-C, HDMI — Omen typically adds a full SD card slot and a secondary USB-C with DisplayPort.

### H2: Gaming Performance — How Do They Actually Play?
**Purpose:** Address core comparison intent — gaming performance  
**Target length:** ~250 words

Avoid raw benchmark numbers (they go stale and competitors have them). Instead, frame around **gaming tiers**:

- **Casual gaming / esports (Valorant, CS2, BGMI):** Both Victus and Omen handle this at high settings with 144fps+. At this tier, paying extra for Omen is not necessary.
- **Mid-tier AAA (GTA V, FIFA, Forza):** Both perform well. Victus RTX 4060 vs Omen RTX 4060 is neck-and-neck. Cooling differences start to show on long sessions — Omen runs cooler.
- **High-end AAA (Cyberpunk 2077, Alan Wake 2, Black Myth Wukong):** Omen's RTX 4070/4080 options create a clear performance gap. Victus tops out at RTX 4060, which handles these at medium-high settings.
- **Thermal sustained performance:** Omen's Tempest cooling design maintains GPU clocks better over a 2-hour gaming session. Victus may throttle slightly after extended load — manageable, not a dealbreaker for most use cases.

**Q1: Is HP Victus owned by HP Omen?**
No. HP Victus and HP Omen are two separate product lines under HP. Victus was launched in 2021 as an affordable gaming series. Omen has been HP's premium gaming brand since 2015. They are not parent/child brands.

**Q2: Why is HP Victus being discontinued?**

**Q3: Which HP series is best for gaming?**
For serious gaming, HP Omen is HP's best gaming laptop line. For gaming on a budget, HP Victus offers the best value. For light gaming on a general-use laptop, HP Pavilion Gaming is the entry option.

**Q4: What is better than HP Victus?**
Within HP's lineup, the HP Omen offers better performance, build quality, and cooling than Victus. Outside HP, comparable alternatives include Lenovo Legion Slim 5, ASUS TUF Gaming A15, and Dell G15 at similar price points.

**Q5: Which should I buy — HP Victus or HP Omen?**
If your budget is under ₹85,000, buy the Victus. If your budget is ₹90,000+, consider Omen — the premium buys you better cooling, higher GPU options, and a more durable build for multi-year use.
